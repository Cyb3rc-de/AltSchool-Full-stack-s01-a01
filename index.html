Biography

My name is Emmanuel Egbe. I am a passionate aspiring developer who is currently based in Abuja, Nigeria. I am dedicated to learning modern web technologies and software engineering principles. I have played around with computers and tech since a very young age but I formally started my journey into technology by learning the fundamentals of web development and having completed an introduction to web development with AI skills course at GOMYCODE, I am continuing my practical learning journey with AltSchool Africa.

I focus on combining software engineering techniques with modern developer productivity tools like AI to build practical, useful and scalable web solutions. My growth as a developer is driven by a deep commitment to consistency, hands-on practice, and the compounding power of small, continuous improvements.

Why I Joined the Program

I joined the AltSchool Africa School of Engineering program, AI-Powered FullStack Engineering, because I want to build a solid foundation in full-stack web engineering, gain hands-on experience with production-grade development standards, and master core concepts such as data structures, algorithms, object-oriented programming and more.

I chose this program to have access to the well experienced instructors and have a structured learning environment that will help me progress from a beginner to a full-stack developer while also networking with like-minded peers. I believe that the program will equip me with the pratices, skills and knowledge necessary to succeed in the ever-evolving world of web standards and technologies.
